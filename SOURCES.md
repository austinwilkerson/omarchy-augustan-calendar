# Quote and calendar sources

Quotes are short excerpts from public-domain texts. Only the author or speaker is displayed in the widget. Initial capitalization and typographic apostrophes are normalized. The collection contains two quotes per theme and rotates deterministically by week, month and year.

- Jesus: Matthew 5:9, King James Version. [Text](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/10/pg10.txt).
- Paul: Ephesians 4:32 (excerpt), Romans 12:21, and 1 Corinthians 6:20 (excerpt), King James Version. [Text](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/10/pg10.txt).
- Ralph Waldo Emerson: “Self-Reliance” and “Friendship,” *Essays*. [Text](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/16643/pg16643.txt).
- Francis Bacon: “Of Studies,” *The Essays or Counsels, Civil and Moral*. [Text](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/575/pg575.txt).
- Benjamin Franklin: *The Way to Wealth*, quoting the traditional proverb under Poor Richard's name. This is an attribution to Franklin's use, rather than a claim that he originated the saying. [Text](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/43855/pg43855.txt).

Calendar structure and observances follow Austin Wilkerson's Augustan Calendar and his supplied 2024 Pocket Calendar. Leap Day follows the creator's confirmed rule: after Augustan Christmas, before the upcoming Gregorian leap February. Easter/Passover, Pentecost, and Crucifixion Remembered are fixed Augustan observances, not Gregorian church-calendar calculations.

The initial high holy days follow the original website: the first day of each regular month. Additional fixed observances follow the pocket calendar: Petras 11, Ionus 40, Andreas 1, Philippos 11, and Thomas 1/4/5. Leap Day is marked separately on Thomas 6. Astronomical equinox/solstice predictions are not included.
